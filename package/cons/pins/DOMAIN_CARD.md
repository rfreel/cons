# XVIII/XXI constitutional repeal slice

The domain is limited to the two identifiers A18 and A21 and one effective
relation: Amendment XXI, Section 1 repeals Amendment XVIII. An operativity
claim for A18 is rejected with A21 as its reason; an operativity claim for A21
is accepted. A18 remains a historical source identifier after repeal.

Authority: https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-21/
The authority establishes the repeal; the Bend program does not establish its
own source facts. This slice does not store the amendment text, decide the
status of other provisions, or model ratification dates, jurisdictions, or
general relation graphs.
